# Story bible

NARRATIVE owns this file. LEAD approves. WORLD does not invent plot.

**Process (non-negotiable):** lock the ending, then work backwards into
payoffs, then into plants. A reversal invented at the moment of reveal will
read as invented.

Status: **M0 draft, ending locked.** Awaiting LEAD approval (issue #14). WORLD
does not write quests or plant `foreshadow:` keys until that approval.

## Locked constraints

- Setting: 1920s-flavored alternate history. Nation **달내 (Dalnae)**. Occupier
  **무쇠 제국 (Iron Empire)**. Underground **새벽회 (Dawn Circle)**.
- Power source: **지맥**. Empire drives **쇠말뚝** into five blood-points.
- Progress gate: Dawn Circle trust `불씨 → 횃불 → 봉화`.
- Three layers, each self-contained: surface revolt / spy mystery / true
  purpose of the stakes.
- No real people, real organizations, or foreign proper nouns (PLAN.md §2.1).
- No cutscenes. Story is told by dialogue, residue on objects, rooms that
  change after events, 한벽일보, broadsheets, radio, rumor.
- Combat is optional. Smuggling and livelihood also buy trust.
- **돌쇠 has no secret and must not gain one.** His death in **검은 밤** is
  the weight. Do not invent a twist, a last letter, or a hidden rank.
- Empire side stays three-dimensional. **류겐 소위** is a principled doubter,
  never a cartoon, and is **not** the mole's handler.

## Ending (locked first)

These four locks are binding. Later acts exist to make them feel inevitable.

### What the five stakes actually feed

The stakes are not cages and not trophies. They are **intake needles**.

Each blood-point is a knuckle of 지맥. A 쇠말뚝 driven there does three jobs
at once:

1. **Extract.** It draws craft, harvest, and civic will out of the peninsula.
2. **Convert.** The draw is piped, as a low iron resonance, toward 한벽성.
3. **Feed.** The pipes meet under **한벽 철탑** — the occupation
   administration's iron tower — and become the intake valve of the
   **무쇠 심장 (Iron Heart)**.

The Heart itself sits in the Empire capital, across the sea. Players never
travel there. What they can reach is the **mouth**: the valve floor inside
철탑. Starve the mouth and the Heart starves.

The Heart is two things the surface revolt cannot see:

- **Weapon.** Foundries, rail, and coastal guns on the continent run long
  past their coal. 한벽일보 will call this "record output" and "the hymn of
  industry."
- **Faith.** The same resonance is broadcast as the **쇠노래 (iron hymn)** —
  a civic radio hour and a chapel drone that makes occupation feel like
  weather. People who live on a drained blood-point go listless. Wells taste
  of iron. Folk arts thin. This is why 곱단 할멈 is sick before any patriot
  has a theory.

청람 선생, in his youth, did not design a weapon. He designed a
**regulator**: five needles to bleed a fevered 지맥 after the famine years.
The Empire kept his measurements and changed the destination. That theft is
the mid-Act-II recovery, not a late invention.

### What 분맥 does if the Empire is cornered

Pull all five stakes and the flow **reverses**. 달내 begins to drink its own
blood again. The Heart misses a beat. The hymn on the radio acquires static.

The Empire's last tool is already in the sockets.

Every stake-hole was cut as a **fuse-well**. 청람's original diagrams marked
them as pressure vents. His former colleagues packed them as **분맥**
charges: if the needles come out, ignite the residual 지맥 at the five
knuckles and cauterize the veins. The primer is a night-long sequence run
from the valve floor in 철탑. It cannot be fired from a field tent.

If 분맥 completes:

- The five blood-point zones lose 지맥 permanently. Arts fail. Harvest rooms
  go barren. 곱단's shrine falls quiet.
- The Heart dies too. Scorched earth. The Empire would rather a dead
  peninsula than a reverse flow.
- This is the **siege fail state**, server-wide. It is not a massacre scene.
  It is aftermath: dry wells, a silent radio, a newspaper that has nothing
  left to lie about.

If the primer is stopped:

- Reverse flow continues. Rooms recover over in-world months, not minutes.
- The Heart goes dark. Liberation is possible.

분맥 is a one-night engineering rite. It rhymes with 검은 밤 on purpose.

### What the tower siege costs

The siege is a server-wide event: a 10-person inner raid on
`hanbyeok:cheoltap` plus an outer battle in the streets of 한벽성. It is
not optional if the server wants a living peninsula. Combat inside the raid
is still only one path; craft and social open gates, hold crowds, and cut
the primer.

**Costs that are always paid** (not player-optional):

- **돌쇠 is already gone.** He died in 검은 밤. Liberation does not refund
  him. The empty stool at 달빛골's smithy is still empty. This is the cost
  the ending cannot bargain.
- **철탑's inner archive burns.** The original regulator diagrams are lost
  unless a raid team chooses papers over a trapped clerk (one inner fork).
  Either way the building does not survive intact.
- **서월향 is wounded** and cannot remain public 백야 after the night. The
  café may reopen. The white lamp does not.
- **달빛골's well stays iron-tainted for a year.** 곱단 said so in Act I.
  She was not being poetic.

**Costs that branch** (write the epilogue and the changed rooms):

| Lever | If honored | If refused |
|---|---|---|
| 한도규 spared and used | He opens the inner lock. Fewer raid casualties. His family can be walked out of the surety house. | The lock is forced. Aftermath rooms show more stretchers. His family remains a rumor. |
| 최만석 flipped (상계 신용 + 새벽회 신뢰 both high enough) | 만석상회 feeds and medicines the outer battle. Street rooms stay standing. | The outer battle starves. Shutters, looted stalls, a warehouse that did not open. |
| 류겐 allied | He opens **동문 (east gate)** on a warrant he writes himself. Lower civilian aftermath. | 동문 is taken. More residue, more rumor, no villain speech. |

No cutscene of the flag. Liberation is a morning room: the radio hymn is
off, 한벽일보 prints a blank masthead box, and the well in 달빛골 is still
brown.

to choices. They are never a credits crawl.

**Always sent**

- **박씨 댁 (돌쇠's mother).** One page. Food, weather, the stool. She does
  not ask what the player did with the Empire. This letter does not branch.
  That is the point.

**최만석 / 류겐 branches**

| Branch | Letter |
|---|---|

| 최만석 tolerated, not flipped | A tax receipt and a denial that he ever chose a side. |

**Other letters (secondary axes)**

- **청람:** kindness vs condemnation at his recovery. One is a lesson plan
  for a new 서당. The other is a returned book with the imperial plate
  scraped off.
- **서월향:** if her name as 백야 stayed secret, a café bill with a lamp
  sketch. If it did not, a travel permit and no return date.
- **곱단:** a charm against rust if 분맥 was stopped; a dry well-stone if
  it was not.
- **안나:** a printed dispatch. Tone matches what she was allowed to see.

## Cast of eight

Every face has a motive, a voice, a knowledge bound, forbidden topics, and
a scheduled recovery. AINPC writes persona cards from this section and
**must not leak a secret before its beat.**

### 청람 선생

- **Public face:** Retired village tutor. Mentor.
- **Private truth:** Former imperial scholar. In his youth he designed the
  five needles as regulators. Guilt turned him.
- **Motive:** Atonement that does not ask to be loved. He cannot un-draw
  the diagrams, so he teaches people to pull what he helped plant.
- **Voice:** Slow, self-correcting. Addresses the player as
  자네. Never shouts. Sample: "말뚝은… 아니, 그건 지금은 말하지 않네."
- **Knowledge bounds:** Village letters, weather, stake *engineering* only
  after II-3. May hint at "fever in the ground." Must not invent Circle
  rosters or 분맥 primer steps before Act III.
- **Forbidden topics:** "I designed them" before mid Act II. Names of his
  old colleagues. The fuse-well reading of the sockets before III-3.
- **Recovery:** **II-3**, at a socket. He recognizes his own cut marks.
- **Notes:** T2 AI NPC pilot in M4. Secret is gated. Fragments in Act I
  only (the flinch, the plate, the old journal under a different courtesy
  name, **월송**).

### 서월향

- **Public face:** Madame of 다방 백야.
- **Private truth:** She is **백야**, head of the Dawn Circle.
- **Motive:** Keep a center so the cells do not become a mob. She took the
  name because no one else would sit still.
- **Voice:** Café warmth, late-hour steel. Uses 우리 as if she means the
  shop. Sample: "그 손님은 차를 안 시켜. 그래도 자리는 있지."
- **Knowledge bounds:** Who drinks what; which guests are Circle (gated);
  달빛골 heat. Never the full peninsula roster — operational security,
  even after recovery.
- **Forbidden topics:** "I am 백야" before I-10. Cell lists. Any claim that
  she ordered 돌쇠 into the night (she did not).
- **Recovery:** **I-10**, after 검은 밤. She lights the white lamp in front
  of the survivors, once, and then the lamp stays dark.
- **Notes:** Café name is hiding in plain sight (FS-005).

### 한도규

- **Public face:** Print-shop typesetter. Comrade.
- **Private truth:** Mole. Wife **한소연** and child **한별** held as
  surety in a boarded guest house in 한벽성 (`hanbyeok:surety-house`).
  Handler is **메스너 주사**, not 류겐.
- **Motive:** Keep those two alive. He hates the work more than his
  accusers will.
- **Voice:** Short shop talk. Over-explains ink and sorts when afraid.
  Sample: "그 오자는 내가 낸 게 아니야. 아니, 그러니까… 활자가 닳아서."
- **Knowledge bounds:** Press schedule, leaflets he himself set, the drop
  protocol (gated). He does not know 무쇠 심장 or 분맥.
- **Forbidden topics:** Family location and handler name before II-8.
  Confession before the player can assemble the three Act I forms.
  Self-exoneration speeches.
- **Recovery:** **II-8.** He does not deny. Disposal is the player's
  (II-9) and writes the letter.
- **Fair-play (Act I, three forms, revisitable):**
  1. *Dialogue* — He asks meeting times he should not need. Café girl
     saw him by the station the night he claimed to be at the press
     (`dalbitgol:cafe-baekya`, FS-002).
  2. *Object* — 주재소 receipt in the ink waste; extra slug of the
     cipher character in his stick (`dalbitgol:printshop`, FS-001, FS-003).
  3. *Newspaper* — 한벽일보 substitution pattern encodes Circle sites;
     errors pause on his "sick" days (`dalbitgol:market` board and
     `hanbyeok:newsroom` archive, FS-001).
  - Coercion (second reversal) also has three Act I forms: a child's
    ribbon in his drawer; wages he will not spend; a half-burned surety
    notice (FS-004).

### 최만석

- **Public face:** Collaborator merchant. **만석상회**.
- **Private truth:** Opportunist. Ideology is a tariff. Can flip into an

- **Knowledge bounds:** Convoys, envelopes, who in the 관아 is hungry.

- **Forbidden topics:** Patriot speeches. Mole accusations against himself

- **Recovery:** **Act III branch** (siege supply). Plants from Act I

### 류겐 소위

- **Private truth:** Growing doubt. He joined to keep disorder from eating

- **Recovery:** **Act III branch.** If the player treated him as a person,

- **Notes:** Empire side stays three-dimensional. His doubt is planted

### 곱단 할멈

- **Public face:** Marketplace mudang.
- **Private truth:** First to feel the ley-line sickness. Prophet of the

- **Motive:** The land is ill and she can taste it.
- **Voice:** Half song, half scold. Sample: "얘야, 그 물은 쇠야. 제사가
  아니라 파이프야."
- **Knowledge bounds:** Wells, weather, 지맥-feel. She can point at five

### 안나 벨로바

- **Private truth:** Third force — international opinion. Not a savior.

## What this bible is not

- Not room prose. WORLD writes rooms against this skeleton.
- Not dialogue trees. AINPC writes persona cards from the cast section and
  will not leak a secret before its recovery beat.
- Not a dump of unused ideas. If it is not scheduled, it is not in this file.
- Not YAML. NARRATIVE does not write `content/zones`.

## Handoff

- **FORESHADOW.md** holds the design-level plants (`FS-001`–`FS-020`).
  Status is `planted` as design. WORLD attaches ids when rooms exist.
- **메스너 주사** is a named supporting face, not one of the eight. He
  must not steal 류겐's doubt plot.
- Zone ids in this file are reservations. WORLD may rename rooms but
  should keep the prefixes (`dalbitgol:`, `meokgol:`, `jaeanam:`,
  `garangpo:`, `huinjaebo:`, `jamseoseong:`, `hanbyeok:`).
