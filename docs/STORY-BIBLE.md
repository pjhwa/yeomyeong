## Locked constraints

- Setting: 1920s-flavored alternate history. Nation **달내 (Dalnae)**. Occupier
  **무쇠 제국 (Iron Empire)**. Underground **새벽회 (Dawn Circle)**.
- Power source: **지맥**. Empire drives **쇠말뚝** into five blood-points.
- Progress gate: Dawn Circle trust `불씨 → 횃불 → 봉화`.
- Three layers, each self-contained: surface revolt / spy mystery / true
  purpose of the stakes.
- No real people, real organizations, or foreign proper nouns (PLAN.md §2.1).
- No cutscenes. Story is told by dialogue, residue on objects, rooms that
  change after events, 한벽일보, broadsheets, radio, rumor.
- Combat is optional. Smuggling and livelihood also buy trust.

## Three-act outline

### Act I — 스며드는 어둠 (불씨)

- Start: 달빛골. Marketplace gendarmerie, a vanished peddler, a newspaper
  with a typesetting error.
- Mentor: 청람 선생 (fragments of a past as an imperial scholar).
- Finale **"검은 밤"**: the network is rolled up in one night. 돌쇠 dies.
  Survivors conclude there is a mole.

NARRATIVE expands beats, locations, and the three solution paths here.

### Act II — 다섯 쇠말뚝 (횃불)

- Five blood-points: ruined mine, burned mountain temple, fogged port,
  border snow fortress, drowned former capital. Each is a party zone with
  its own faction fight.
- Mole line pays off: 한도규, coerced, family held. Disposal is a player
  choice that writes into the Act III epilogue.
- Clues already exist in Act I (typesetting pattern, ink stain, broken alibi).

### Act III — 여명 (봉화)

- Stakes were never only oppression — they feed the Empire capital's weapon
  and faith, the **무쇠 심장**.
- Pull all five and the flow reverses. Cornered, the Empire prepares **분맥**.
- Server-wide siege of the Government-General tower (10-person raid + outer
  battle). Liberation. Letters afterwards.
